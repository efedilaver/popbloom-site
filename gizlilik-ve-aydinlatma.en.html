Popbloom Privacy Policy and Data Processing Notice

Version 1 · Last updated: 2 November 2026

This notice explains what happens to your personal data when you play Popbloom. It is also written to give the information required by Article 10 of Turkish Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

In short

  • We do not ask for your email address, real name, date of birth or contacts, and we do not ask for location permission. Server logs contain an approximate location derived from your IP address.
  • Your game data is kept on servers in Germany so that we can run the game. We do not ask for separate consent for this.
  • Ads only play when you choose and are not personalized: they aren't chosen by your interests and your advertising identifier isn't used.
  • Your data regularly leaves Turkey. Section 5 says where it goes and why.
  • You can delete your account inside the game at any time.

3. How we collect data

Fully automatically and electronically: the game sends data from your device to the server, you type into fields, Apple passes on sign-in, purchase and refund information, Google confirms the video you watched, the server infrastructure writes logs by itself, and the ad software passes data to Google. In support messages, you write the data to us yourself.

4. Ads

Ads only play when you choose, as rewarded videos. Each time you open the game, we do not start the ad service until you choose to watch a video. After that, the game may request ads from Google while it is open so that one is ready.

Through its ad software, Google may collect: IP address, device identifiers, ads you have seen, interactions such as video views, performance information such as app launch time, and crash logs.

Ads are not personalized. Your advertising identifier is not sent to Google, Apple's permission prompt is not shown, and non-personalized ads are requested. Google still processes data to show and measure the ad and to prevent fraud; it says it does not use this data to build an ad profile in this case.

Google acts on its own decisions with this data, not on our behalf (independent controller). The number we give Google is a random number made for each video, not your account number. Details: Google's explanation.

In the European Union, the United Kingdom, Switzerland and some US states, Google's consent form may appear; the video may not open until the form is answered. You can change your choice under Settings > Privacy > Ad privacy options. You can report an inappropriate ad under Settings > Privacy; the prepared email states where the ad was and when you watched it.

7. Deleting your account

Delete it inside the game under Settings > Account > Delete account. If Apple is linked, you will be asked to sign in with Apple once more. Guest accounts are deleted the same way.

  • Deleted right away: your account, names, game state, session record, our copy of the notification token and your list of running jobs, your ad choice record, your one-time code, prism movements other than purchases and refunds, and the save, cache and session on your device. We also remove the Apple authorization; if we cannot reach Apple, the authorization may stay, we tell you, and you can remove it in your phone's settings. Your progress and remaining prisms do not come back; we warn you first if you have prisms left, and link to Apple's refund page.
  • Stays for a while: server logs at most 7 days, backups at most 7 days. The rewarded ad record stays for up to 90 days with its link to your account removed.
  • Stays: Purchase records stay for the period in section 6, with your account number removed. Because the transaction number remains, this record is not anonymous; it can be matched with Apple's record. The deletion record stays for 3 years.
  • Outside our control: the notification token at Expo, data at Google and Apple.

If you also played on another device, the save there stays until the game opens on that device and learns the account was deleted.

Removing the game from your phone does not delete your account. The session may stay in your phone's secure storage and return when you reinstall. If you play without linking Apple and lose your device, your progress and prisms may be lost. In that case there is no way to prove the account is yours, so we cannot restore it for you.

8. Your rights and requests

Under Article 11 of the KVKK you can: learn whether your data is processed and who receives it, have it corrected or deleted, ask for recipients to be told, object to a result against you that comes only from automated analysis, and ask for compensation for damage.

You can correct names and delete your account in Settings. For other requests:

  • Inside the game: Go to Settings > Privacy > Data request, create a code and send the prepared email. This is the easiest route and it confirms your identity. The code is valid for 30 days and works once; a new code cancels the old one.
  • By post: The postal address for written requests is shared by email on request
  • By email: e38756034@gmail.com. Include your player ID from Settings and the one-time code you create in Settings; otherwise we cannot confirm the account is yours.

We settle your request free of charge within 30 days at most. If we refuse, we give the reason. Requests may be made in English or Turkish. If we do not reply or you find the reply inadequate, you can complain to the Turkish Personal Data Protection Board within 30 days of learning of the reply and in any case within 60 days of your request.

9. Children under 13

Popbloom is not designed for children under 13 and is rated for ages 13 and up on the App Store. We do not ask for age, so we do not know how old a player is. A parent or player who learns that a child under 13 is playing can write to us by the routes in section 8. Including the player ID, or otherwise the Apple order number and group name, lets us find the account. Once we find the account and confirm the report, we delete the account and its data as described in section 7.

11. Security

The server connection and disk are encrypted, and each player can only read their own row. We do not write game state or names to server logs. No system is completely secure. If there is a breach, we notify the Board and affected players. Because we do not know your email, we notify players inside the game through a game update and at https://efedilaver.github.io/popbloom-site/gizlilik-ve-aydinlatma.en.html.
