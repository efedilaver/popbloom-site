We give you a personal, non-transferable permission to use the game for personal, non-commercial purposes.

2. Your account and the risk of loss

The game starts with a guest account, and linking an Apple account is optional. If you do not link Apple, you may lose your progress and the prisms you bought when you lose your device or delete the game. In that case there is no way to prove the account is yours, so we may not be able to restore it. Linking Apple reduces this risk.

You can delete your account under Settings > Account at any time. Deleted progress and prisms do not come back. If you have prisms left, we warn you first.

3. Prisms

  • Prisms are in-game virtual currency. They give you a right of use. They are not cash outside the game and cannot be sold or transferred to anyone else.
  • They do not expire while the game is running.
  • Purchases are made through Apple, which takes the payment. Refund requests go through Apple's process.
  • The starter pack can be bought once per account. There is a limit on the purchased prisms an account can hold; a pack that would exceed it is not sold at that time.
  • If Apple refunds a purchase, we deduct those prisms from your balance. If you already spent them, your balance may go negative; your next prisms cover the shortfall first.
  • If prisms you bought did not arrive, write to us with your player ID from Settings and your Apple order number.
  • If you are under 18, ask a parent or guardian before buying.

Watching rewarded videos is optional. The ad choice you make does not change the reward.
